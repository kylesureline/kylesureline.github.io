RecipeVaultApp · MERN · AI ingestion

RecipeVault — a recipe platform with AI ingestion

The problem
Turning a pile of unstructured recipe sources into clean, usable data is manual work.
What I built
A MERN app with an AI ingestion pipeline that parses and normalizes recipes automatically, image storage on S3, MongoDB Atlas, and push notifications — built as an offline-first PWA for home cooks.
Why it matters to you
It runs on a real CI/CD pipeline — automated tests, staging, then production, in Docker. That’s the difference between a demo and something you can hand a client.

Ask me for the technical walkthrough — I’ll show you the pipeline, not just the UI.
